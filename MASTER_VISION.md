# MASTER VISION: PROJECT ALETHEIA 2.1

Methodology version: `TF-2D/1.0`
Approved by the principal researcher: 2026-10-18

## The core tension

The longitudinal study examines the relation between fidelity to knowable truth
and the construction of narrative order. Public communication necessarily
organizes events into intelligible accounts; the research therefore asks how
strongly an account is framed and how faithfully it remains tied to the
available evidence. It analyzes observable texts and trajectories. It does not
attempt to read a journalist's mind or infer private intent.

## Core outcome

Every item or approved aggregation is located on a two-dimensional `T × F`
map:

- `T` — truth fidelity: alignment with time-indexed anchors, source
  transparency, fidelity to uncertainty, and correction over time;
- `F` — frame intensity: the salience, coherence, and organization of an
  interpretive frame.

`T` and `F` are independent core axes. They are never added, inverted, or
weighted into one score. A high `F` is not evidence of truth, quality, bias,
malice, or misconduct, and is neither good nor bad by itself.

## Diagnostic boundary

`N` is retained only as an edge-case diagnostic. It may flag a case for human
review when a severe, concrete, text-observable departure from the evidentiary
record can be identified. It cannot establish intent, malice, or conscious
deception and does not enter rankings, central case comparisons, or any
composite result.

Historical `A` and `N` fields remain preserved as `legacy/audit data` so that
past runs can be reproduced. They are not current research outcomes. The
historical 65/35 formula is retired.

## Research scope

The design supports comparison across contested, evolving events and across
domestic, Western/international, and regional media strata. Four cases are
closed and require no rerun. Cases 005–007 have passed Gate 1 and remain in
basket construction pending alternative-source decisions; none has passed
Gate 2.
